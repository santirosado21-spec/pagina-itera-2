## Problem / evidence

Label: WHY ITERA

Headline: Your team already uses AI. Nobody's checking their judgment

Body: Prompting is the part everyone teaches. The harder part is choosing what to hand over in the first place, and judging what comes back. That's what we measure.

## Product capabilities

### Catch the cost before it ships
Short practices built on real artifacts: the made-up number in the draft, the customer data pasted into a prompt. Your team catches it before it goes out. Every practice ends in an action, not a quiz.

### Simulate real work
“The email your manager asked for”, “urgent incident”, “price objection”. Your team makes the calls with AI in the flows they already own.

### Measure what changed
A manager dashboard: who is ready, where the gaps are, which risks showed up. Assign the targeted practice and re-assess to see what moved.

## How it works

This must be the protagonist and should communicate this full chain visually:

Company → Roles → AI Simulations → Evaluation → Adoption Data → Skill Gaps → Personalized Learning → Manager Dashboard → Measurable AI Adoption

### 1. Start with the assessment
Your team runs a live case built on your own work. Fifteen minutes, real artifacts, a decision at the end. Adapted by role: sales, marketing, finance, operations.

### 2. They practice five minutes a day
Itera assigns the practice for each gap. Streaks and team goals keep the habit. People come back because it's short, not because it's mandatory.

### 3. You see what changed
Readiness per person and across the team, the risk events behind it, and the practice each gap unlocked. Download the report as a PDF.
